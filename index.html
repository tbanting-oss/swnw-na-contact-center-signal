North America's Contact Center Market Is Splitting Into Two Very Different Races

On-premises still has a clear leader. Cloud doesn't — yet.

North America's contact center market — CCaaS and on-premises combined — sits at roughly ~$3.7B–$3.9B in 2025. Split it in two, though, and the competitive picture changes completely. On-premises is a legacy stronghold: Avaya alone holds close to a third of it. CCaaS has no equivalent leader — the top three players are separated by single digits, and over a third of the market sits outside the named top five entirely.
